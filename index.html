The story

We're not trying to make just another coffee.

BearBrew started with a simple thought: coffee is already part of the way people power through their days, so why not rethink the format?

The vision is a ready-to-drink black coffee with electrolytes that feels as good in your hand as it fits into your routine — at a desk, between classes, after a workout, on a shoot, during a late night or on the way to the next thing.

We're building BearBrew from the ground up in India, with product, taste, packaging and community being developed step by step.

FAQ

Before the first sip.

What is BearBrew? +

BearBrew is a ready-to-drink black coffee with electrolytes, currently being developed in a 300 ml format.

Is it available to buy? +

Not yet. BearBrew is currently in development. Follow @drinkbearbrew for launch updates.

Why electrolytes? +

Electrolytes are part of BearBrew's product concept. Final quantities, nutrition information and functional claims will be published after formulation and testing are finalized.

Is it zero sugar? +

The current packaging concept says “ZERO SUGAR.” Keep this public claim only after the finished formulation and final regulatory labeling support it.
